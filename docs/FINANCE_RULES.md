# Finance rules

All stored monetary values are integer toman. A transaction amount must be positive. Opening balances represent money already held before tracking begins and do not count as income. Negative opening balances and transactions that would overdraw a source account are rejected by the API.

Account balance = opening balance + sum of incoming cash transactions and gold sale deposits − sum of outgoing cash transactions. A transfer is one record with source and destination; its effect on total account balance is zero. Monthly income and expense totals include only their respective transaction types, based on the transaction date in the configured Tehran time zone. Monthly net cash flow = income − expense.

Expected payments, unpaid bills, and unpaid debt principal do not affect account balances or actual income/expense totals. An expected payment can be deposited in an active household account; each partial receipt creates exactly one income transaction using its income category. A bill payment creates one expense transaction using its expense category. Each partial settlement must be positive and cannot exceed the remaining amount. Outgoing settlements cannot overdraw their source account. Settlement requests use a unique idempotency key to avoid duplicate posting on retries. Recorded status changes from pending to partial to settled; overdue is calculated using the Tehran-local current date, so a partially settled item can also be overdue.

Repayment of principal on a simple debt record uses `debt_receipt` or `debt_payment` transaction types. These affect account balances but are excluded from income and expense totals. Simple debt records do not calculate interest; use the loan feature when an interest rate is part of the agreement. New obligations and their settlement history are immutable through the API in this version; record a new item for corrections.

Loans are separate from the simpler debt records. The optional origination account posts the actual borrowed or lent principal as a `loan_borrowed` or `loan_lent` cash transaction; leaving it empty tracks a pre-existing loan without moving cash again. Neither principal origination nor principal repayment is income or expense. A blank interest rate means no interest. With a positive rate, the user supplies a maturity date or a monthly schedule; total fixed simple interest is `round_half_up(original principal × annual rate × term days / 365)`, with the rate expressed as a fraction of 100. There is no compounding, late fee, or automatic recalculation for early payoff. Payments are allocated to the remaining interest first, then principal. Actual paid interest is an income transaction for lent loans or an expense transaction for borrowed loans in the `سود وام` category. Remaining interest is shown separately and is not added to net worth until received or paid.

Optional equal monthly installments are generated on Persian calendar months from the first due date, with the last installment absorbing integer-toman rounding. Repayments fill the schedule in due-date order. Unpaid installments before the Tehran-local current date are overdue; future unpaid installments are upcoming. A schedule is a plan and never posts cash automatically. Loan repayment requests require an idempotency key and cannot exceed the remaining balance or overdraw the payment account.

Generic assets use the current manually estimated value. Each named gold purchase is a separate holding. Its current value = remaining grams × the saved Navasan 18 karat price per gram, rounded down to an integer toman. Remaining invested cost is allocated proportionally from the original purchase cost; unrealized profit/loss = current value − remaining invested cost. A sale reduces the holding's remaining weight and adds the proceeds to the selected account. It is an asset conversion, not income or expense. Partial sales reduce the holding's remaining cost basis proportionally.

The `gold-price-updater` Compose service fetches Navasan's `18ayar` value and saves it for each household every 12 hours. It makes one API request per refresh regardless of household count. Keep `NAVASAN_API_KEY` in the untracked `.env` file; do not commit it. [Navasan's guide](https://www.navasan.tech/webserviceguide/) documents the endpoint as HTTP with the key in the query string, so the API key is sent without transport encryption. The 18 karat rate is the valuation reference for all named holdings in Phase 1.

Net worth = sum of all account balances, including inactive accounts with remaining money, + active generic asset values + current gold value + remaining loan principal owed to the household (from both debt records and lent loans) − remaining bills and loan principal the household owes (from both debt records and borrowed loans). Expected future income and unpaid loan interest are excluded. This treats bills as liabilities when recorded, while monthly income/expense totals remain cash based. Entering the same initial cash in an account and as an asset will double-count it; accounts should represent liquid money and assets should represent other wealth.

Insights uses actual dated income and expense transactions grouped by Persian calendar month over the inclusive selected range, up to two years. Category comparison uses the immediately preceding period with the same number of days. Its upcoming/overdue summary shows unpaid bills and expected payments separately from completed cash flow; upcoming means due within 30 Tehran-local days. Net-worth history reconstructs dated account flows, gold sales, principal repayments, and outstanding obligations/loans. Historical generic asset values and gold prices were not recorded, so older points use the current value and current 18-karat price. The chart is explicitly an estimate, not a historical valuation ledger.

Alerts use Tehran-local dates. An unpaid bill or installment is upcoming when its due date is within the user's 0–90 day lead time, and overdue after that date. Partial loan repayments are allocated to the oldest scheduled installments first for alert purposes. An expected payment alerts only after its date passes while a balance remains. High spending compares a category's current Jalali month to the same elapsed number of days in the previous Jalali month; it requires at least 100,000 toman in the baseline, a 50% increase, and a 100,000 toman absolute increase. Alerts do not affect financial balances or totals. History remains after settlement or preference changes.
