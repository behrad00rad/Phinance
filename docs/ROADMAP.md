# Roadmap

## Implemented

Household-scoped authenticated API, accounts, categories, transactions, transfers, generic assets, named gold holdings and partial sales, twice-daily Navasan 18 karat price refresh, expected receipts, bills and debts with partial settlements, borrowed and lent loans with fixed interest and monthly schedules, an Insights page, in-app rule-based alerts and history, dashboard summaries, Persian mobile interface, Docker development setup.

## Next

Household invitation and membership management; edit/correction workflow for transactions, obligations, and loans; recurring bill schedules; push/email alerts and scheduled background alert evaluation; amortizing or variable-rate interest and early-payoff recalculation; historical asset/gold valuation snapshots; gold purchase as an explicit account-to-asset conversion; simple asset editing in the UI; backup/export of the SQLite database.

## Later

Property tracking, rental income, additional gold purity and coin pricing, monthly net-worth snapshots, budgets, analytics, CSV/Excel export, backups, shared family accounts, parent/admin mode, recurring transactions, notifications, and PWA installation.
